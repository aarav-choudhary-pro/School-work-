########## SLIDE 5 ##########
[Google Shape;162;p17] Incorrect single-core bundling creates hazardous circulating currents and severe tray overheating.
[Google Shape;163;p17] INSTALLATION GEOMETRY
[Google Shape;164;p17] Laying Arrangement: 1-Core vs Multi-Core
[Google Shape;165;p17] Single-Core Cables
[Google Shape;166;p17] • Trefoil Formation: Route in grouped trefoils to balance induced magnetic fields.
[Google Shape;167;p17] • Single-Layer Rule: Strictly no multi-layer stacking; space groups by 2 × Cable Diameter.
[Google Shape;168;p17] • Short-Circuit Cleating: Rated cleats every 1.5 m to withstand dynamic electromagnetic burst.
[Google Shape;169;p17] • Non-Magnetic Clamps: Stainless steel or aluminum hardware to stop eddy heating.
[Google Shape;170;p17] Multi-Core Cables
[Google Shape;171;p17] • Flat Side-by-Side: Low-voltage power placed touching or spaced by 1 × Cable Diameter.
[Google Shape;172;p17] • Thermal Stacking: Avoid multi-layer stacking unless ampacity derating is applied.
[Google Shape;173;p17] • Direct Tie Fastening: UV-resistant heavy nylon or coated steel ties on rungs.
[Google Shape;174;p17] • Vertical Rung Anchoring: Support multi-core every 450 mm on vertical risers.
